# Text size findings

## October 9 follow-up

The current 64-case audit passes with `A11Y_STRICT=true` at 320×568 and
375×812, with 1×, 1.5×, 2×, and 3× text. The before run recorded 39 overflow
errors across 20 cases; the after run records no overflow errors and no
constrained-scroll-viewport warnings.

Compare `build/ux_overflow_before/index.html` with
`build/ux_overflow_after/index.html`. Resources have a separate visual audit at
`build/ux_resources_after/index.html`, generated by
`flutter test test/accessibility/ux_resources_capture.dart`.

The fixes let check-ins and category buttons grow naturally, move sheet actions
to wrapping bottom footers, and keep long study acknowledgments in the reading
area. On short screens with large text, the recording category/profile header
scrolls with the form while navigation and the primary action remain reachable.
Profile names can wrap, and the whole name/chevron control is tappable.
The shared short-screen dialog also uses an actual fullscreen surface, avoiding
the default side margins that squeezed the calendar on a 320px phone.

The history scenario now scrolls until its first lazy entry is built, rather
than assuming a fixed scroll distance. It still asserts that the entry exists
and runs the same expanded-entry checks. Overflow errors remain strict failures.
Separate regression tests cover consent gating, profile selection, answer
selection, Today actions, contextual help, and the terms renderer.

Truncation and fitted-text signals still appear in the report and remain visual
review prompts; passing this audit is not a claim that every app state has been
tested.

## Historical baseline

Audit run: September 30, 2026. **48 cases completed; 31 contained confirmed
overflows, at 12 distinct reported source locations.** The report contains 504
screen captures, including intermediate scroll positions. Counts describe the
fixtures in this run, not the percentage of the entire app that is inaccessible.

Open `build/text_size_audit/index.html` for the evidence. Filter to a screen and
device to compare scales. “First observed” below means the smallest tested
factor that failed; the precise breakpoint may lie between sampled factors.

Follow-up: detail rows now share an adaptive question/answer view, with a saved
**Automatic / Question above answer** preference in Account → Reading preferences.
Pain rows, ordinary answers, and transit details use that view; pain-location
chips can wrap vertically. The current report includes 16 additional detail
gallery cases (64 total). The original counts below are the pre-fix baseline;
use the generated report for current results. Other batches remain open.

The Study screen demonstrates why a clean Flutter overflow log is insufficient:
at 375px/3× the instruction scroll area is only **22px tall**. At 320px/3× it is
**0px** and the containing column also overflows. The audit flags the constrained
viewport even where no content overflow is reported.

## Review alongside those fixes

- Profile/history headings shrink through FittedBox as text grows. Decide how
  to preserve the requested readability while keeping the existing logo/header.
- Sticky day headings ellipsize dates/counts at larger sizes. Keep the divider
  conditional on pinning, and prioritize a readable date over secondary counts.
- Calendar day numerals shrink inside their cells. Review those captures in
  addition to fixing the reported header overflow.
- The pain slider's upper endpoint (`10`) truncates in some recording captures.
- Review the active-filter and submit captures visually even when no error is
  reported. Intentional ellipsis and ordinary offscreen content are not automatic
  defects; essential answers and actions need stricter treatment.

Promote each resolved behavior into a focused regression test, then rerun its
audit case in strict mode. Use the full matrix for changes to shared controls.
The other layout findings are retained for subsequent batches.
